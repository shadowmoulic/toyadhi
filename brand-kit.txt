# TOYADHI AGRITECH - BRAND KIT (Tentative)

## 🎨 Color Palette
- Primary Background: #070908 (Dark Obsidian)
- Secondary / Action: #3B82F6 (High-Tech Blue)
- Action Hover: #2563EB (Deep Blue)
- Muted Text: #A1A1AA (Cool Gray)
- UI Highlights: #059669 (Emerald Green - specifically for Success/ROI indicators)
- Glass Effect: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03) with 20px blur

## 🔡 Typography
- Main Sans: 'Outfit', 'Inter', sans-serif
- Headings: Bold, clean, geometric
- Spacing: Tight line-height (0.95) for H1, generous spacing for body text (1.6)

## ✨ Design Principles
- Cinematic Immersive: Heavy use of dark backgrounds and radial overlays.
- ROI-Centric: Actionable buttons are high-contrast blue (#3B82F6).
- Trust & Authority: Clean glassmorphic borders and rounded corners (8px for icons, 32px for sections).
- Smart Modular: Components follow a modular "Node" logic.

## 🖼️ Visual Identity
- Logo: Sprout node in a rounded white tile.
- Imagery: High-resolution agritech photography with brightness filter (0.6) and dark gradients.
