“You have land → we turn it into a profitable system”

🔥 FUNNEL FLOW (simple but lethal)
1. ENTRY (Traffic Pages / Ads / Social)

User enters from:

Hydroponics cost
Polyhouse profit
Farming business ideas

👉 They are in exploration mode

2. HOOK (Above the fold)

Immediately hit with:

“Want to know how much your land can earn?”

CTA:
👉 Get Free Farm Profit Estimate

NOT consultation. Too early.

3. QUALIFIER (Smart Lead Capture)

Form asks:

Land size
Location
Budget
Interest (hydroponics / polyhouse / etc.)

👉 This is GOLD data

4. VALUE DROP (Instant)

After submission:

Show:

“Your estimated farm potential”

Even if rough:

Investment range
Expected ROI
Suggested model

👉 This builds trust instantly

5. AUTHORITY STACK

Then show:

Case studies (₹ → ₹)
Systems implemented
Logos / trust

👉 Now they believe

6. HARD CTA (Now they’re ready)

“Get a Detailed DPR + Execution Plan”

Options:

Book call
WhatsApp
Download DPR sample
7. FOLLOW-UP (this is where money is made)
WhatsApp automation
Email sequence
Retargeting ads
